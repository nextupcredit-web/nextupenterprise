Where to Start

Don't open a letter template first.
Read these sections first.

Most people who fail at DIY credit repair start writing letters on day one. The guide is built in a specific order — follow it and the process works. Skip ahead and it doesn't.

01–06
Read Before You Write

Sections 1 through 6 are your foundation. Pull your reports from AnnualCreditReport.com, save dated PDFs, and run the audit in Section 4 before touching a letter template.

07–10
Build Your File & Dispute

Gather your ID and documentation. Send letters via certified mail — not online portals. One per bureau, not all three in one letter. Wait 40 days before following up.

15–16
Build While You Repair

Section 15 is the one people skip. It's the reason two people can run identical disputes and end up 200 points apart. Use your 90-Day Plan in Section 16 as your calendar.

"The leverage you have is accuracy, not volume. Find what is genuinely wrong, prove it, and pursue it patiently." — Section 17
