03

Compare tradelines across bureaus. Every field. Every account. This is where disputes come from.

FCRA § 611, § 623, and FDCPA § 1692g — four tools, four different jobs. Match the tool to the target.

The Honest Version
"The core idea in one sentence: you are not asking the bureaus for a favor — you are holding them to an accuracy standard the law already requires of them, and documenting it when they fall short."

There is a version of this guide that would have sold you harder. It would have told you that every negative item can be deleted, that bureaus are legally required to remove anything you challenge, and that thirty days from now you would be a different person financially. That guide sells well. It also gets people into trouble.

The credit reporting system makes real, documented, provable mistakes — wrong balances, accounts that belong to someone else, dates that were quietly changed, debts that should have aged off years ago. The law gives you specific, enforceable tools to make them fix those mistakes. This guide teaches you those tools and how to use them properly.

What it will not do is teach you to challenge accurate information by pretending you don't recognize it. That approach gets your disputes flagged as frivolous, wastes months, and can cross into misrepresentation. Dispute what is wrong, precisely, with proof. It works better anyway.
