FAQ

No fluff. Straight answers.

How fast will I see results? +

Many clients start seeing changes within 45 days. Most complete the program in 4–6 months. Every file is different — we’ll be honest about what to expect.

Do you guarantee results? +

No — and any company that does is lying. What we guarantee is a real, consistent process done the right way using actual consumer protection laws.

Can you remove everything? +

Not everything is removable. But anything inaccurate, outdated, or unverifiable can be challenged — and that’s exactly what we go after.

What’s the difference between the service and the DIY Kit? +

The done-for-you service means we handle everything every month. The DIY Kit ($47) gives you the exact same letters and strategies to do it yourself. Both work — the service just saves you the time.

Do you help me build credit too? +

Yes — and this is a big part of what makes us different. We guide you on building your profile so when items come off, your score actually grows.
